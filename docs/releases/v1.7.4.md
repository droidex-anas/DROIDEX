## What's faster

- **Smoother streaming with many agents at once.** The app now does a fraction of the work per streamed update — measured at two-and-a-half to seven times fewer screen updates depending on layout, and up to eleven times fewer in heavy eight-agent sessions. Streaming, scrolling and switching stay responsive where they used to stutter.
- **Better battery life.** Animations pause in parts of the window you can't see — scrolled-out rows, folded sections, panels hidden behind others — and the window's visual effects sleep while the app is in the background.
- **Lighter on your network.** Remote sessions compress their traffic, status polling adapts its cadence instead of spinning, and failing connections back off instead of retrying in a storm. Noticeably kinder on a metered or tethered connection.
- **Snappier project views.** Loading kit chips, review diffs and version history no longer rescan more than they need to.

## What's new

- **Answer a question in the composer's place.** When an agent asks, the question comes to where you type, the keyboard focus follows it, and your answer returns you to your draft. In the transcript, an answered question now shows its answers.
- **A calmer compact transcript.** A running turn folds into a single Working line, with compaction and subagent cues kept clean inside the fold.
- **Arrow-key navigation in pull request tabs.**

## What's fixed

- **The sidebar restores after a reconnect.** Losing the connection no longer leaves the session list empty when the bridge returns.
- **A notification click during reload is kept.** Clicking a notification while the app reloads now waits for its chat and lands you there.
- **MCP server settings recover** after the background service restarts, and the Claude command list retries after a failed first fetch.
- **Usage numbers are more accurate.** Child agents' usage lands on the child, retired providers count their final usage, and malformed readings are rejected instead of distorting totals.
- **Failed spawns settle properly.** Agents that fail before starting no longer linger as stuck or stale, and background tasks report as paused rather than failed when stopped.
- **Memory stays bounded** in long-running sessions with many child agents.
