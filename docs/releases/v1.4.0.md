## What's new

- **Projects run like a team with one lead.** The project's main chat is the lead, and its word is final for every thread. Threads never wait on you: the lead answers their questions and approves what is within its own access. A thread's report reaches the lead even while it is busy, and the lead wakes up when a thread reports, asks, fails or goes quiet, or when a reminder comes due. It sleeps otherwise.
- **Nothing in a project gets lost.** Reports, questions and reminders survive restarts, pauses and stops. Any thread you have not read stays marked unread until the lead acts on it. A stopped or finished thread picks up where it left off when the lead sends it a message, with its conversation and worktree intact.
- **Answer approvals and questions from the Threads panel.** A thread waiting on you shows its request right in the panel, so you can allow it or answer without opening the thread.
- **Pause and resume a whole project in one step.** Pause stops every thread's turn; Resume picks each one up from where it stopped. Stop on the lead stops only the lead.
- **Leads keep a plan, to-dos and reminders**, and a clearer set of tools: every message they send says exactly what happened to it (steered in, started, resumed, or queued and its place in line).
- **Change autonomy while a turn is running**, on Droid, Claude Code and Codex. Lowering it takes effect at once.
- **Steers feel instant.** A message you send into a running turn appears as your bubble right away, the work it lands in stays in view, and you can take a steer back to edit it until the model picks it up.
- **The browser agent's cursor stays visible** for the whole turn, moves along a smooth arc and names each step as it goes.
- **Side chats** open faster on Codex, close for good when you close them, and a hidden one is always one click away.

## Signing

DROIDEX is now signed with its own stable certificate. macOS will ask you to approve permissions such as the microphone **one more time** after this update. From then on, updates keep them.

## Fixed

- **Connection retries no longer flood the chat.** A harness reconnecting several times now shows as one row with the number of attempts.
- A Stop that the harness confirms late no longer marks a newer turn as stopped.
- Messages sent to Codex while it works now arrive in the order you sent them, and Codex and Droid confirm them as soon as the model has them.
- The "Session runtime released after 30 minutes idle" notice is gone. An idle chat still frees its memory and comes back as soon as you send a message.
