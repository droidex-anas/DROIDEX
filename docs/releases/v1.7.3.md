## What's new

- **New chats have a backdrop.** Until a chat starts, a dithered backdrop sits behind the composer, lit in the color of the harness you pick: orange for Droid, terracotta for Claude Code, blue for Codex. Switching harness cross-fades the light, and the first message fades it away. Choose Glow, Rotor, Ridgeline, Contour or None in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **A logo above the greeting.** Show the harness logo, which follows the harness you pick, the DROIDEX wordmark, or nothing.
- **Your own backgrounds.** Add up to eight images and keep them as backgrounds. Show them as photos, or turn on **Dither your images** to redraw them as halftone dots in the harness color. Remove one from its preview.
- Backdrops and logos work in light and dark themes.
