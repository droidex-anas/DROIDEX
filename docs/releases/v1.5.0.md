## What's new

- **Remote: use DROIDEX from any browser.** Pair another computer or a tablet and run the full DROIDEX app on it: projects, chats, settings, diffs, Git and terminals. Agents, keys, history and files stay on your laptop. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted through a relay that only sees ciphertext. No account, VPN or open port needed.
- **Set up in three steps.** Claim a username, link an authenticator app, and scan the pairing code with your other device. Every new browser needs a fresh authenticator code and your approval on the laptop, and you choose how long the laptop trusts it: 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or ask every time. Each browser is named by what it runs on, such as "Safari on iPad (iPadOS 26)" or "Chrome on Windows", so you can tell them apart.
- **Clear states on both sides.** A browser shows right away when the laptop is offline and reconnects by itself when it is back, including after the laptop sleeps. It says so when the laptop declines it, and a browser you revoke or disconnect loses access at once and stays out until approved again.
- **Profile.** Settings → Profile shows your token totals, a daily, weekly and cumulative activity chart, streaks, API-equivalent cost, and which harnesses and models you use most. It covers Droid, Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, including work done outside DROIDEX, and token counts match `ccusage`.
- **Share your profile.** Publish a signed public page at `droidex.vercel.app/public/<username>`. It shows only aggregate activity and harness and model names: no prompts, paths, sessions or costs. Unpublish at any time.
- **Search worktrees.** The "Work in" picker has a search field. Type any part of a worktree or branch name, in any order, and press Enter to pick the top match.
- **Files as chips.** Read and search activity in a chat shows the files it touched as chips you can click to open in Review. Parallel reads merge into one row, and long lists fold behind "+N more".
